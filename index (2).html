The real problem

Understanding English is one thing. Speaking it is another.

كتفهم الإنجليزية، ولكن ملي كاتجي تهضر كتبلوكا؟

I understand every word. Then someone asks me a question.

I translate in my head, and the conversation moves on.

My heart speeds up the moment I have to speak.

I know the vocabulary. I just never get to use it.

I studied the grammar, so I stay quiet instead.

That's not a you problem. It's a practice problem.

English for your real life

Not just English lessons. Real-life skills.

الإنجليزية اللي غادي تحتاجها فحياتك اليومية.

University & academic life

Join the discussion, not just the lecture.

“Could you clarify what you mean by that?”

For seminars, group projects and emails to professors.

Career & job interviews

Say what you can do, clearly.

“Let me walk you through my experience.”

For interviews, meetings and your first week at work.

Travel & international communication

Find your way, and make friends on the way.

“Excuse me, which way is the airport shuttle?”

For airports, hotels, hostels and the people you meet.

Everyday conversations

Small talk is a big skill.

“Do you want to grab a coffee later?”

For new friends, online games and chatting with anyone.

Personal development & online learning

Unlock the internet in its original language.

“I'm working on my pronunciation every day.”

For courses, videos, communities and your own goals.

Your progress starts with one step

  1. Tell us your goal

    University, work, interviews, travel. Whatever English you need first.

  2. Start where you are

    No perfect English required. Together we choose a sensible first step.

  3. Practice a little, often

    Short, regular practice beats long, rare sessions. Build the habit first.
